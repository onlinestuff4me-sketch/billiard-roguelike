The rate

A saved stroke pays 500 × room number. In room 2 that is 1,000 and barely registers; in room 12 it is 6,000 and beats most pots. Efficiency becomes more valuable exactly as it becomes harder.

Doors

The exit choice you already have, with one new reward type in the pool: +1 stroke for the next room. It is the strongest pick in the deck late, and nearly worthless early — which is what makes it a choice.

If you fail

Run out of strokes with the contract unfilled and the rack breaks loose: every ball still standing takes a bite out of your hull. That is what a static table can still threaten you with.
