The earned power

Freeze is not how the game works.
It is what you win.

Between strokes the table is already frozen, so there is nothing to slow down. The thing worth earning is the other freeze — stopping the table mid-stroke, while the cue ball is still travelling, and re-aiming from wherever it has got to.

Plan · no clock

Plan

Static rack. No timer, no drain, nothing walking at you. Read the whole table and commit.

Resolving · live

Resolve

Release and physics takes it. The 4 is away, but the cue is drifting onto a mine and you have no say.

Freeze · 2 left

Freeze

Spend a charge: the table stops dead. Re-aim off the cue ball where it sits and release again — no stroke spent.

Earn it on the table

A freeze cell sits somewhere on the felt. Shoot the cue ball into it and you carry 3 charges forward. It costs you a stroke and a good position to go and get it — that is the trade.

Earn it at the door

Freeze is also a boon offer between rooms, competing head-on with the multiplier and stroke-refund picks. Taking it means not taking those.

Why here

Freeze-and-re-aim is the best-feeling thing in the game, so it makes a poor default and a superb prize. Moving it behind a cost also means the base game can be honestly, calmly static — the tension moves into the stroke budget where it belongs.
