Three pairs a new player cannot tell apart

Each row is two things that do completely different jobs and currently look alike. Mostly the fix is form, not colour: in row one both halves genuinely double your shot, so sharing a hue is right — one of them just has to be a hole and the other has to be open. Row two is the exception, and there the hue really was lying.

And one thing that should stop existing

The shatter gate — a beam between two posts that destroys a ball driven through it and pays 60% of a pot — is a fourth family of its own: you pass through it like a pad, but a ball that arrives leaves the table like a pocket. That contradiction is not worth a name. It becomes a pocket out on the felt: same dark void, same solid rim, drawn hexagonal rather than round so its position on open felt reads as deliberate. One family fewer, one rule fewer, and the 60% rate disappears with it.

That leaves exactly four words for everything on the table — ball, pocket, pad, wall — and the felt pocket is the thing that used to need a fifth.
