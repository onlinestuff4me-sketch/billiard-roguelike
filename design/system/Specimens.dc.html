Two notes for whoever builds this

Every glyph must survive being small. These are drawn at about 55 screen pixels on a phone, over a bloomed table. Each one is a single closed silhouette with no interior detail finer than a tenth of its width — the warning triangle is the only pointed shape on the felt, and the double is the only one that is type rather than a drawing.

Amber solids sit next to nothing. The old red solids were twenty degrees of hue from hazard red, and on a bloomed table that gap closed. Amber is far from both mint and red, so the balls no longer borrow meaning from the semantic palette — which is what lets red mean only danger.
