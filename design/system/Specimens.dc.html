The one adjacency to watch

Danger orange-red #ff5a3d sits about twenty degrees of hue from solid-ball red #ff3d6e, and on a bloomed table that gap narrows. Form carries it — a mine is flat and dashed and has no number, a solid is raised and numbered — but this is the one pair worth watching in playtest. If it reads badly on a phone, push danger toward pure orange rather than moving the balls: the ball hues are the part of the palette players already know.
